Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

This policy describes the consumer health data that the OCRecipes app collects, why, who receives it, and the rights you have over it. It is written to meet the Washington My Health My Data Act and similar state laws in the United States, and it applies alongside our Privacy Policy. OCRecipes is operated by Family Meal Planning Services (“we”, “us”), based in Alberta, Canada.

“Consumer health data” means personal information that is linked to you and identifies your past, present or future physical or mental health status. It includes health information we work out from other things you tell us.

The health data we collect, and why

We collect the health data below only if you give your consent in the app. Without it, we do not ask for or store these details.

CategoryWhat it isWhy we collect it and how we use it
Health conditionsConditions you select in your profileTo fit nutrition targets, recipe suggestions and AI coaching to you
MedicationWhether you use a GLP-1 medication, which one, and when you startedTo adjust nutrition guidance and AI coaching
Diet typeThe diet you say you follow, such as vegetarian, keto or low FODMAPTo filter recipes and suggestions and fit AI coaching to you
Allergies and intolerancesThe allergens you list and how severe each isTo warn you about ingredients and filter recipes and suggestions

Food records and messagesFoods you log and scan, and what you write to the AI coach, which may include health information you choose to mention. Coach saves short notes from these conversationsTo run the features you asked for and fit later answers to you
Inferred moodOne running score, worked out by AI from your messages to the coach. It fades back to neutral within daysSo the AI coach can adjust its tone. It receives the score only as “low”, “neutral” or “good”

Where the health data comes from

  • You: what you enter in your profile, log, scan, or write to the AI coach.
  • Our own processing: the mood score and the coach’s notes are worked out from your messages by the AI models listed below.

The health data we share

We do not sell consumer health data. We share it only to produce the answer or feature you asked for, and AI features run only after you allow them in the app. The categories shared are: health conditions, medication, diet type, allergies and intolerances, body measurements, activity and goals, the mood band, the coach’s notes, your food logs for the day, and the messages and photos you send to an AI feature.

Who we share it with

We have no affiliates. We share consumer health data with these categories of third parties, each of which handles it for us under its terms of service:

  • AI model providers: OpenRouter, which passes each request on; Microsoft Azure, which runs the OpenAI models we use; and Google (Vertex AI), which runs the Gemini models we use. They receive the categories above when you use an AI feature.
  • Recipe search provider: Spoonacular receives your diet type and intolerances based on your allergies, with your search words.
  • Hosting and database provider: Railway stores the information in your account on our behalf.

The full list of our service providers and what each receives is in our Privacy Policy. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

Your rights and how to use them

  • Confirm and access. You can ask whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, and get a copy of it, with a list of the third parties we shared it with and how to contact them. In the app: Profile → Settings → Export My Data.
  • Withdraw consent. Profile → Settings → Withdraw health-data consent removes your health details and stops their use. Profile → Settings → AI features stops all sharing with the AI model providers.
  • Delete. Profile → Settings → Delete Account deletes your account and the health data in it. You can also ask us to delete health data without closing your account.

You can make any of these requests by emailing our Privacy Officer at support@ocrecipes.com. We may need to confirm that the request comes from you. We answer within 45 days. If we need more time we will tell you why, and take at most 45 days more. Requests are free.

Appeals. If we refuse a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer or emailing the same address with “Appeal” in the subject. We will tell you in writing what we decided and why, within 45 days. If the appeal is refused, you can complain to your state’s Attorney General. In Washington: atg.wa.gov/file-complaint.
