Delete your OCRecipes account

This page explains how to delete your account for the OCRecipes app, made by Family Meal Planning Services. You can do it in the app, or ask us by email without opening the app.

Ask us by email

Email support@ocrecipes.com with the subject “Delete my account”.

  • Send it from the email address on your account, and tell us your username if you know it.
  • We reply to the address on the account to check that the request is yours. Once you confirm, we delete the account.
  • If you can no longer use that address, or you signed in with Apple and hid your email, tell us and we will find another way to check it is you.

We delete the account within 30 days of your request. Asking is free.

Or delete it in the app

Open OCRecipes and go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. You confirm with your password, or with Google or Apple if that is how you sign in. The account is deleted straight away.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently deletes your account, your data, your recipes, and the images you uploaded. It cannot be undone.

What we keep

  • With nothing that links them to you: the store’s transaction number for a purchase, with its product and refund state, and product nutrition data that your label scans helped verify.
  • For a limited time: request logs, which carry your account ID, and error reports.
  • A recipe picture made for you in a chat that had already been deleted can remain in our image storage.

Our Privacy Policy has the full list of what we collect and how long we keep it.

If you have a subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play or the App Store, where you bought it.

Delete some data, keep the account

You can ask us to delete part of your data without deleting your account. Email support@ocrecipes.com from the address on your account and say what you want deleted.
