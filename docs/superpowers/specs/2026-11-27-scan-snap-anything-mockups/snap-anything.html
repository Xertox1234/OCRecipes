4 · What each answer says

Meal → "Looks like a meal ✓" → Photo Food Log result · Nutrition label → label result · Menu → menu result · Raw ingredients → "Looks like ingredients ✓" → Cook & Track · Grocery or restaurant receipt → "Looks like a receipt ✓" → opens the receipt camera (receipts can run to several photos, so you snap it there) · Barcode → the barcode flow (above) · Not food → "Couldn't tell what this is". Menu, Cook & Track and receipts are premium: when locked, Continue shows the upgrade card, as today.
